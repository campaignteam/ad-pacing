How to read the results
Pacing % compares actual spend against expected spend. A result of 100% means the campaign is on pace. Variance is actual spend minus expected spend. A negative variance means underspending, while a positive variance means overspending.

Projected Final estimates the spend at the end of the flight if the average daily spend continues. Budget Used shows the percentage of the total budget already spent. The status uses the selected pacing tolerance. Campaigns that have not started, have invalid dates, or have no expected spend yet will be marked Pending.
